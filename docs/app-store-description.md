## Description (170 words)

Discover your interior design style with Cova — the AI-powered room redesign app. Take a quick style quiz by swiping through beautiful rooms, then capture your space with your iPhone camera. Cova's AI transforms your room into a stunning redesign that matches your unique style blend.

Browse the result in an immersive 360-degree panoramic view. Arrange 3D furniture in your room, then shop real products from top retailers like Wayfair, Amazon, IKEA, and CB2 — all matched to your style and budget.

Chat with your AI design advisor for personalized recommendations. Pin your favorite products directly into your next render. Save designs, compare before and after, and redesign as many rooms as you want.

Whether you're moving into a new home or refreshing your current space, Cova makes professional interior design accessible to everyone. Your dream room is one swipe away.
